# Trees, plants and mood (2026-10-21)

Evidence file (write once). Owner ask: "based on research first. Redo plants and trees. Love the
leafs effect on trees, add some on ground too. Let's give it a better mood."

## Sources (community; no first-party Roblox guide on foliage style)
- DevForum, low-poly / stylized trees: full canopies from a few rounded or slightly concave
  shapes rather than many hard cubes; smooth shading suits organic shapes; vary the shapes so no
  tree is symmetric; bake a colour gradient into the leaves.
  https://devforum.roblox.com/t/low-poly-tree-feedback/366051 ,
  https://devforum.roblox.com/t/feedback-on-stylized-trees/2137703
- SIGGRAPH 2014 poster (stylized trees after Eyvind Earle): light the canopy by direction, brighter
  toward the light and on the rims, darker underneath.
  https://history.siggraph.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/12/2014-Poster-97-Murphy_Stylized-Trees-and-Landscapes.pdf
- Stylized tree packs (Fab, Sketchfab): canopies coloured by one gradient, the mobile-friendly way.
  https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/stylized-tree-e0dca4534717410a8b8f4500181e98c4
- Stylized painting practice: fallen leaves and small clutter on the ground under trees tie a tree
  to its ground. https://www.proko.com/@siriusvictoria
- DevForum lighting guides: Atmosphere is the biggest single mood lever; ColorCorrection only a
  little (contrast and saturation about +0.1 to +0.3, never the tint); SunRays weaker than default;
  a warm time of day turns the rays orange; over-processing looks worse than none.
  https://devforum.roblox.com/t/development-tips-better-lighting/580976 ,
  https://devforum.roblox.com/t/guide-to-lighting/2764787 ,
  https://devforum.roblox.com/t/good-warm-lighting/2128280

## What we take
1. Canopies as clusters of the shared low-poly ball (80 triangles at any size), coloured by
   height: deep at the bottom, pale at the sunlit top. Replaces the rotated cubes.
2. Keep the falling leaves the owner likes; add leaves on the ground: a few flat ones lying under
   each tree and a low emitter that slides leaves along the grass.
3. More species for colour: sakura (pink), ginkgo (gold), maple (red), soft firs, flowering bushes.
4. Mood through Lighting (owner saves it in Edit): Atmosphere first, a little ColorCorrection,
   softer sun rays; options shown as captures before anything is saved.
