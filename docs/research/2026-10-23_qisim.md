## 2. The sim

The game's own code runs (Config, Main's economy functions, Shop's grant); what Main does inline is
mirrored with a line check that fails the run when the source changes. The player follows the HUD
arrow, otherwise picks the best payback (qi per qi/s, read from the game's `rate()`), walks the real
pad distances, takes orbs, gifts, pill, chest, streak and the shop gift, buys karma nodes, and recruits
with the qi above the rebirth price before rebirthing (disciples are kept, qi is not).

Profiles (GameAnalytics 2025 Roblox benchmark, thousands of titles: sessions a day median 2.0, P75 3.5,
P90 6; session median 6.6 min, P75 14.5, P90 26.4): median 2 x 7 min, engaged 3 x 15, hooked 4 x 26;
regular 2 x 30 is the old sims' player.

## 4. What it says (7 days, median of 5 seeds, tuned on save A)

| Player | Rebirth 1 | Rebirth 5 | Rebirth 10 | End (realm, life) | Waiting |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| median 2 x 7 min, free | 11.5 min | - | - | 4, 5 | 1.5% |
| median, passes | 11.7 min | - | - | 6, 5 | 0% |
| engaged 3 x 15 min, free | 11.3 min | 1.8 h | 4.8 h | 5, 11 | 2.2% |
| engaged, passes | 10.8 min | 1.5 h | 4.5 h | 12, 11 | 0% |
| hooked 4 x 26 min, free | 10.7 min | 1.7 h | 4.2 h | 14, 20 | 5.9% |
| hooked, passes | 10.6 min | 78 min | 3.5 h | 10, 21 | 6.8% |
| regular 2 x 30 min, free | 10.9 min | 1.9 h | 5.0 h | 10, 13 | 1.1% |
| regular, passes | 10.8 min | 1.5 h | 4.5 h | 5, 14 | 0% |

Waiting = idle seconds with nothing at all to buy (not even a recruit). "End" is where the last life
stood, so it swings with where the rebirth fell.

1. Nobody waits in week 1 up to realm 13: at most 4% in any realm (one 12% blip at realm 10).
2. The wall is realms 14 to 16, which only hooked players reach (~10 h in): 20 to 50% waiting there,
   with or without passes.
3. Short-session players live on offline qi: for 2 x 7 min a day it is ~90% of their qi (free or
   with the passes). Their pace is set by the offline cap (4 h) and rate (0.5).
4. The passes bring rebirths ~15 to 25% sooner (rebirth 5 at 1.5 h vs 1.8 h for engaged) and take
   waiting to zero before realm 12.
5. Recruits are usually the biggest qi sink, since recruiting the qi above the rebirth price is the
   best use of it (disciples stay, qi does not).
6. Orbs depend on the Array, the last realm-1 building, rebuilt every realm, so they bring far less
   than the old sim said (it counted 32 to 41% of all qi).
