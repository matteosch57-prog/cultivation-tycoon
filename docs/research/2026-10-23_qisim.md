## 2. The sim

The game's own code runs (Config, Main's economy functions, Shop's grant); what Main does inline is
mirrored with a line check that fails the run when the source changes. The player follows the HUD
arrow, otherwise picks the best payback (qi per qi/s, read from the game's `rate()`), walks the real
pad distances, takes orbs, gifts, pill, chest, streak and the shop gift, buys karma nodes, and recruits
with the qi above the rebirth price before rebirthing (disciples are kept, qi is not).

Profiles (GameAnalytics 2025 Roblox benchmark, thousands of titles: sessions a day median 2.0, P75 3.5,
P90 6; session median 6.6 min, P75 14.5, P90 26.4): median 2 x 7 min, engaged 3 x 15, hooked 4 x 26;
regular 2 x 30 is the old sims' player.

## 4. What it says (7 days, median of 5 seeds)

| Player | Rebirth 1 | Rebirth 5 | Rebirth 10 | End (realm, life) | Waiting |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| median, free | 9.5 min | 1.6 h | - | 3, 6 | 6% |
| median, passes | 8.7 min | 76 min | - | 4, 7 | 0.5% |
| engaged, free | 9.2 min | 1.7 h | 3.5 h | 10, 14 | 7% |
| engaged, passes | 9.3 min | 65 min | 2.6 h | 11, 16 | 3% |
| hooked, free | 9.0 min | 1.7 h | 3.7 h | 12, 21 | 22% |
| hooked, passes | 8.3 min | 61 min | 2.5 h | 16, 23 | 20% |
| regular, free | 9.2 min | 1.8 h | 3.8 h | 7, 16 | 12% |
| regular, passes | 9.3 min | 64 min | 2.5 h | 8, 18 | 10% |

Waiting = idle seconds with nothing at all to buy (not even a recruit).

1. The first hours have almost no waiting: at most 15% in every realm up to 11, for every player.
2. The wall starts at realm 12 (after about 4 h of play): free players wait 12 to 65% of the time in
   realms 12 to 16, paying ones 11 to 76% in realms 12 to 18. That is where a hooked player would get bored.
3. Short-session players live on offline qi: for 2 x 7 min a day it is 70% of their qi (98% with the
   passes). Their pace is set by the offline cap (4 h) and rate (0.5), not by what they do in-game.
4. The passes make rebirths come ~40% sooner (rebirth 5 at ~1 h vs ~1.7 h) and cut early waiting to
   almost nothing.
5. Recruits are usually the biggest sink (about 50 to 95% of qi spent in most runs), since recruiting
   the qi above the rebirth price is the best use of it.
6. Orbs depend on the Array, the last realm-1 building, rebuilt every realm: in one measured run it stood 36% of
   play time, so orbs are 1 to 24% of qi (the old sim said 32 to 41%).

Old sim (sim_game.py regular, 3 days): rebirths at 9, 37, 78, 108, 144, 178 min; qisim: 9, 31, 64,
83, 108, 126, 150, 174 min. They agree early; qisim gets ~30% more rebirths by hour 3.
