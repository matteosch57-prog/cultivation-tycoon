**Pattern:** the visit mechanics that work best are **physical objects at the other base** (a bucket, crops, a stall) that **pay both sides**, are **capped per base per day**, and give a separate bonus for **breadth** ("visit N different houses"). Adopt Me's trick-or-treat is the closest precedent to "bless a sect", and the most-played: symmetric 15/15, once per house per day.

## B. Classic neighbour-help patterns: numbers, abuse, fixes

- **Caps are per target per day.** FarmVille allowed 5 actions per farm per day ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FarmVille)). An academic summary says you can visit a given neighbour once a day, so more neighbours means more visits ([Wohn, ACM SRC](https://src.acm.org/binaries/content/assets/src/2010/d.yvettewohn.pdf), search summary). Tapped Out adds a **global** daily cap (50) on top, with **tapering** rewards ([tstoaddicts](https://tstoaddicts.com/2016/06/16/superheroes-sequel-101-neighbor-visits-social-prize-calendar/)).
- **Hay Day:** an old patch note says you can ask for help with truck/visitor orders 3 times a day plus one extra neighbour help per day ([APKMirror 1.40.98](https://www.apkmirror.com/apk/supercell/hay-day/hay-day-1_40_98-release), search summary, may be outdated).
- **Gift caps:** "1 gift per neighbor per 4 hours" plus a "daily gift cap to prevent farming". Stardew allows 2 gifts per NPC per week. Sender pays nothing ([DEV Community playbook](https://dev.to/truongpx396/the-social-games-playbook-2i51), read; an informal blog).
- **Helping carries social meaning:** players read fertilizing a neighbour's crops as a sign of general kindness, and the system runs on *delayed reciprocity* ([Wohn](https://src.acm.org/binaries/content/assets/src/2010/d.yvettewohn.pdf); [ResearchGate: Using your friends](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/241623768_Using_your_friends_Social_mechanics_in_social_games)).
- **Abuse:** "alt accounts feed currency to a main account". Fixes: IP/device/behaviour alt detection, trade taxes, soulbound items, "auto-flag, manual review" rather than auto-ban ([playbook](https://dev.to/truongpx396/the-social-games-playbook-2i51)). Lumber Tycoon 2 gates transfers on **account age of at least 1 week** plus a cooldown ([allthings.how](https://allthings.how/roblox-lumber-tycoon-2-how-to-get-and-send-money/)).
- **Backlash:** FarmVille made players choose between "spamming their friends" and paying. Jonathan Blow said it encouraged "users to exploit their friends" ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FarmVille)).

**Applied to a 6-player server:** with a maximum of 5 other sects, alt farming means one person running a second account on the same server. Cheap fixes:
1. A per-pair cap: once per (giver, receiver) per day.
2. The reward is a **small % of the receiver's own income rate**, not a flat amount, so an alt with no income gives a main almost nothing.
3. Pay **both** sides, so there is no one-way funnel.
4. Optionally, a minimum account age or play time before blessings count.
5. Reward **breadth** (bless 3 different sects) over repetition.

## C. DevForum: encouraging visits, and dislike of forced social

- "The majority of players want to interact with other players and possibly friends rather than play alone." The same poster also said that single-player tycoons have worked well, and another suggested letting players choose their style ([DevForum multiplayer tycoon thread](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/is-it-a-good-idea-to-add-multiplayer-to-a-tycoon-game/3621257), read).
- Advice to add social features as "a reason to stay", to offset how static tycoons are ([DevForum: Is making a tycoon worth it?](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/is-making-a-tycoon-worth-it/456568), search summary).
- Social systems fail through **poor discoverability** (players never found the party system) more than through lack of interest ([DevForum social features feedback](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/requesting-developer-feedback-for-social-features/122476/102), search summary).
- Forced social play: games that "force interaction in exchange for loot and power" frustrate players ([Massively OP](https://massivelyop.com/2017/03/16/massively-overthinking-when-social-play-in-mmos-is-predatory-by-design/), search summary). The recurring complaints are progression locked behind a group and penalties for playing solo.
- Implementation note: put any visitor-triggered reward on the server with an owner/visitor check. One tycoon thread shows a Touched button firing several times in a millisecond and paying out up to 4x ([DevForum](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/tycoon-button-grants-income-boost-multiple-times-in-a-millisecond/4815673)).

**Implication for a calm game:** keep the blessing a **bonus, never a gate**. A solo player on an empty server must not fall behind noticeably. The FarmVille lesson is to never make the request the mechanic (no "ask X to bless you" prompts). Let the walk be its own invitation.

## D. What makes the arrival feel good

From the precedents above:
- **A physical thing to interact with at the other base** (Adopt Me's porch bucket that **goes dark** once used, giving clear state; FarmVille crops). For a sect, this could be a shrine or incense burner at the sect gate that the visitor bows at with a ProximityPrompt.
- **Visible identity and showcase**: owner name and portrait over the base, plus a "Featured" spotlight (Tiny Town Tycoon). SimCity Social's visit shows "everything they have built", and the act it offers is a **compliment**.
- **Owner feedback**: the receiver is notified (SimCity Social / FarmVille sent notifications on each exchange; the playbook recommends a notification when a gift arrives). In-game, this means a toast ("X blessed your sect, +qi") and an effect at the owner's shrine.
- **Persistent trace** (a design inference, not from a source): a blessing counter or guestbook plaque at the shrine ("Blessed 132 times"), and a lit incense stick per blessing today, so a visit leaves a visible mark.
- **Breadth reward**: Adopt Me's "visit 15 houses" pumpkin and Tapped Out's prize calendar suggest a small daily bonus for blessing every sect on the server.

## Recommended shape for "bless a sect" (synthesis)

Walk to a sect and press the prompt at its shrine. **Both** players get a small qi grant worth a few minutes of the **receiver's** own income (each side scaled to its own rate). Limits: once per pair per real day, no self-blessing, server-validated, and only within N studs of the shrine. The owner gets a toast plus a shrine effect. The shrine shows a lifetime count and today's incense. Optionally, a small extra for blessing all sects on the server that day. Never prompt or ask players to bless.
