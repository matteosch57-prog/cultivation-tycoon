# Step-on pads, previews and build-ins: what top tycoons do (2026-10-21)

Web research for the pad redesign. Sources were thin: no wiki or DevForum thread breaks down the pads of
Military / Super Hero / Pet / 2 Player Millionaire Tycoon. Tags: **[SRC]** a page read, **[SNIPPET]** a
search summary only, **[KNOWLEDGE]** genre knowledge, not verified.

## Shared conventions
- Step-on pads belong to the "dropper tycoon" line (Zednov / Berezaa kits and their heirs); Retail Tycoon 2,
  Restaurant Tycoon 2 and Miner's Haven use build menus instead [KNOWLEDGE].
- A flat or slightly raised disc, a billboard with name and price above it; Robux pads in another colour
  (gold or purple) [KNOWLEDGE].
- Green when affordable, red or grey when not (UEFN Button Manager has affordable / unaffordable materials)
  [SRC] https://uefncentral.com/verse-library/button_manager
- The pad disappears on purchase, the pads that depend on it appear; next pads fade in [SRC/SNIPPET]
  same URL; https://devforum.roblox.com/t/any-clue-what-to-call-this/806171
- Debounce by removing the pad at once [SNIPPET]; accidental Robux buys from pads are a real complaint
  (controller A = jump and confirm) [SNIPPET] https://techraptor.net/gaming/news/roblox-scammers-are-abusing-controller-bindings
- Build-in: per-part tween from an offset (rise from below, or scatter then assemble), staggered ~0.03 s,
  force the final CFrame on Completed [SRC] https://devforum.roblox.com/t/tycoon-animations/833635
- Pacing: a pad at 2 to 5 minutes of current income; over ~10 minutes of waiting is too slow [SRC]
  https://www.creation.dev/learn/how-to-make-tycoon-roblox

## Not documented anywhere (room to stand out)
- A preview of the building before buying: pad tycoons usually show nothing until the buy.
- An "affordable now" ping, hold-to-buy rings, sound layering for the build-in.

## Game feel literature applied to a step-on buy
- Nijman (Nuclear Throne): small camera kick, a flash circle on the first frame, layered impact sound,
  permanence [SRC] https://infovore.org/?p=5275. Applied: the pad sinks under the foot, a tiny nudge (no big
  shake), click then coins then building thuds; the building is the permanent evidence.
- Juice it or lose it: easing, squash and stretch, restrained particles; overdone juice that does not fit
  the setting costs immersion [SRC] https://newsletter.gamediscover.co/p/has-your-game-got-juice-and-more
