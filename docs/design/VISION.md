# Vision

**Pitch:** build your cultivation sect on a floating peak above a sea of clouds. Walk onto pads to raise
halls, gardens and towers, recruit disciples, and break through to the next realm.

**Audience:** calm, phone-first Roblox players who like tycoons with steady small rewards.

## Pillars
1. **Always something to buy.** 3 to 5 pads open at any time, each with a visible reward.
2. **Watch it grow.** One Main Hall upgraded over 5 levels; the island itself grows twice.
3. **Everything answers back.** Sound, motion and light on every action ([FEEL](FEEL.md)).
4. **Fair and calm.** No combat, no stealing; Robux buys speed and comfort, never random rewards.

## The loop
Qi flows every second from buildings -> walk onto pads to build -> more qi/s -> features (recruiting,
pills, orbs, slots, expansions) -> all built -> **breakthrough**: the peak resets, income and costs
multiply, disciples stay.
