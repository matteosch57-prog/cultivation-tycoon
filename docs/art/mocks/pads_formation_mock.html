Step-on pad mock: three feels

Pick a concept, then press Walk up and buy. The disciple walks in from 24 studs: the building preview wakes up as they come close, then they step on the pad and the building appears. Sizes are in studs like the game. Sounds are synth stand-ins; the real ones come from the Roblox library.

A · Jade Seal

Pad
A raised jade seal in a stone ring. It breathes softly when you can afford it, and clicks down under your feet.
Preview
Jade hologram of the building, a light band sweeping up through it.
Appears
Rises from the ground floor by floor, a wood tick per floor climbing in pitch, then a little bounce, sparks and a chime.
Cost
About 300 triangles per pad.

B · Lotus

Pad
Eight petals around a glowing seed pod. Open and swaying when you can afford it, a closed bud when not. Petals snap shut around your feet, then burst open.
Preview
Its footprint drawn on the ground, the ghost hovering just above it.
Appears
Pieces drop from the sky one by one, each landing with a thud and a puff of dust.
Cost
About 250 triangles per pad.

C · Formation

Pad
A carved array with two rune rings turning in opposite ways. Your step spins them up and they snap inward with a flash.
Preview
The building floats in pieces, pulled apart. The closer you walk, the tighter it gathers.
Appears
The floating pieces snap together in a spiral, then a flash and a bell.
Cost
About 450 triangles per pad.

Every concept can borrow a part from another (for example the pad of A with the preview of C). The far-away beacon beams stay as they are. Drag on the scene to turn the camera.
