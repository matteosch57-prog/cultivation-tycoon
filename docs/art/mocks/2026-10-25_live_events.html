Restocks and Weather

Grow a Garden brings players back with two clocks: a shop that restocks every 5 minutes with random, limited stock, and weather events that give anything grown during them a mutation worth more. Here is how each would look in Cultivation Tycoon. All numbers are first drafts; both sims set the final ones before anything ships.

Option A

Wandering Merchant (Grow a Garden seed shop)

  • A merchant stall in the hub, and a Merchant tab in the shop. Restocks every 5 minutes, on one world clock, so every server gets the same stock at the same moment (like Grow a Garden).
  • Each restock rolls every item: is it in stock, and how many. Common scrolls are almost always there; an Immortal Scroll shows up about once every 3 hours.
  • Scrolls summon a disciple of that exact rarity (not random), paid in qi, priced as minutes of your income. Limited stock per player.
  • A rare restock shouts to the whole server: "Immortal Scroll in stock!" Players start checking the timer.

Option B

Heavenly Weather and mutations (Grow a Garden weather)

  • The sky changes for the whole server: rain, a thunder tribulation, a blood moon at night, a starfall. Each lasts 2-3 minutes, with a banner and real rain, lightning or falling stars.
  • Disciples recruited during the weather can come out mutated, like Grow a Garden crops: a coloured badge, a glow on the platform, and more income. The odds show on the recruit odds sheet while it lasts.
  • Every mutation is a new index entry, so collectors have a long-term goal beyond building the same peak again.
  • New players get their first Spirit Rain about 4 minutes in, right where 18% quit while recruiting.
  • The Spirit Tide (x2 qi) and the Tide Bell stay as they are.

Option C · my pick

Both, linked, the way Grow a Garden runs them

  • A and B together. The weather can bring special stock: a Thunder Scroll (an Elder that is always Thunderborn) only sells during a tribulation, like Grow a Garden's seeds that only show on certain restocks.
  • That gives two reasons to stay a few more minutes ("rain in 1 min", "restock in 2 min") and two reasons to come back.
  • Build order: weather first (it plugs into the recruit loop new players already use), then the merchant.
Trading, later

Grow a Garden's trading only works because millions of players want each other's pets. At 1,600 players, with a friend in the server making no difference to returns yet, trading would mostly be an empty booth and a new place for scams and duping. Mutated disciples are exactly what people would trade, so B and C set it up for later.
