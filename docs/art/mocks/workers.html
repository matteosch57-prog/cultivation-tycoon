Sect Workers

Outer disciples you hire with qi. Each one works at one building, makes that building earn more, and makes the peak feel alive. Nothing here is built yet.

1. How you hire one pick one

A. Hire pad by the building my pick

A small pad next to the building. Step on it, a worker walks in. Same as every other pad, no new screen.

B. Workers button

A HUD button opens a list. Buy from anywhere, but it is a new screen with more text.

C. Free with the building

Workers just come with the building. Pure life, nothing to buy, no extra qi.

2. Up to 3 per building the dots on the card

1 hired

2 hired

3 hired: pad gone

Each hire adds one worker you can see and +50% to that building's qi. After the third the pad disappears.

3. Who works where gold = first batch

4. The look pick one

A. Plain work clothes + straw hat my pick

Grey and brown, a straw hat, a tool. Never mistaken for a rare disciple, and light on phones.

B. Disciple robes, white tier

The same avatars as the meditating disciples. Prettier, but they blur with the ones you collect.
