Drag any pond to turn it. Every new option also fixes the water: it fills the whole circle under the rocks, its ripples are cut to the circle instead of a square patch, and a paler shallow band runs along the edge (the round floor and circle-cut ripples are how the Gladiator RNG fountains were fixed).

Eight flat blocks in a ring, a raised disc of water, a square ripple patch in the middle.

A · Stacked stones

Each rock is 2 or 3 blocks and wedges tilted and half sunk (the DevForum way to build rocks from parts). Slate material, three grey tones.

About 30 parts per pond, nothing to upload.
B · Carved bouldersRecommended

Real rounded rocks: 4 low poly rock meshes, each reused at its own size and turn, with pebbles in the gaps. Same mesh trick as the trees, so they draw cheaply.

About 20 rocks per pond. Needs 4 small meshes uploaded once (your OK).
C · River stone shore

A low ring of flat river stones with three big boulders as accents, and a darker deep middle. Calmer, more garden than wild.

About 30 stones per pond. Same 4 meshes as B.
