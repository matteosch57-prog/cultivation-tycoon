Sect Island Layout

How the buildings on a peak could be grouped, joined by roads, and how the island could grow piece by piece. Nothing here is built yet.

What the study says

Buildings in districts

City and tycoon builders group buildings into districts with a clear job; neighbours that belong together give each other a bonus (Block By Block Tycoon, City Tales).

Land grows plot by plot

Theme Park Tycoon 2 and RollerCoaster Tycoon buy the next plot of land beside the park, never the whole ring at once.

Roads and landmarks

One main avenue, a round plaza and curving side paths read better than scattered buildings; a tall landmark at the end pulls players in.

Today on our peak

Resource and feature buildings are mixed around the rim, only the Stone Path is a road, and each expansion grows the whole circle.

A. Districts and a ring road my pick

Four districts around a round plaza: Spirit Fields (left), Alchemy and Training (right), Disciples (back left), the Main Hall at the end of the avenue. Each expansion adds one lobe of land where its district grows.

District bonus: every qi building in the same district adds +5% to the others there, so where you build matters.

B. Mountain terraces

One stair avenue climbs three terraces to the Main Hall. Expansion 1 adds a wing on each side, expansion 2 adds the summit terrace at the back.

Terrace bonus: buildings on higher terraces earn more, and the climb shows your progress from the bridge.
