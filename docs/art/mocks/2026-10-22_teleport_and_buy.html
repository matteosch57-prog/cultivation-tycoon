Teleports + purchase moments

Tap a button on each picture to try it. Pick one letter for each part.

1. Teleport buttons

AFlank the qi

House left of the qi drop, trophy right of the karma. Same round chips as the gift and pill. Grow a Garden puts its Garden / Sell / Seeds jumps at the top centre too.

BCorner pair

Two small square chips top left, beside Roblox's own buttons, above the Shop. Keeps the qi counter alone at the centre.

COne tray

Both chips in one dark tray, top right above the gift and pill. Reads as a travel menu.

On tap (all three): squash + click + buzz (Toy.feel), a quick jade wipe, and you land facing your gate or the ranking wall. A short cooldown stops spam.

2. After a Robux purchase

Disciples (Elder, Immortal, ... Primordial) play the recruit reveal you already have: the orb charges up to its rarity and bursts into the card. These three are for passes, qi packs and the pill.

ASpotlight

Dark veil, slow gold rays, the item pops in with Toy.reward (white blink, ring, motes, chime, double buzz), its name in gold, then it flies to where it lives. Qi packs pour drops into the counter.

BQuick burst

No veil, about one second. The item bursts at the centre, then flies straight to its HUD spot, which pops. Never stops play.

CImperial scroll

A red and cream scroll unrolls with the item, a red seal stamps it with a thud, then it rolls up and the item flies to the HUD. Most xianxia, a bit slower.
