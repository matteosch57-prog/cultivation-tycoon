How should a click feel?

Tap each button. Every pick adds a press sound and a phone buzz (Roblox UIClick haptic). Pick one for all buttons, and one for claim moments.

ASquish

Sinks onto its shadow and squeezes on touch, springs back with a small overshoot. Sound and buzz on touch, not on release, so it feels instant.

BSquish + glint

A, plus the face flashes white for a blink and a thin ring pops off the edge on release.

CJelly

Squashes wide on touch, then wobbles tall and settles like jelly. The most playful; Pet Simulator style.

ClaimReward moments

For Claim, chests, gifts. Pick one. Both use a stronger buzz and the chime.
