## How the tiers step up
- Tier 1: a plain steel or jade jian, a soft white wind trail, a faint jade or white glow.
- Tier 2: a gold ornate blade, a blue qi swirl trail, gold rings or flames.
- Tier 3: a glowing legendary blade, a gold or lightning energy trail, a full aura pack.

## Notes before building
- R12: delete every script on insert (all picks show 0), rebuild from the mesh ids where possible.
- R16: phones. Tier 1 picks are 334 to 2,399 triangles; prefer the lowest that reads well in a Play capture.
- Aura packs: take one ParticleEmitter set from the pack and drop the demo dummy.
- Votes are near zero on most of the store; `Glowing Light Sword` (80 up), `Axoie's Auras` (98 up) and `Aura Pack Premium` (88 up) are the rated ones.
- Nothing here is a fake light (R3): auras and trails are effects on the sword, the owner's call for paid cosmetics.
